# AFTER THE SIP — production direction

A single luminous bronze accent leads us from coffee into molecular scale and returns us to the cup. The image fills the vertical frame. Text changes position, scale, and spatial role across the film rather than occupying a fixed title area.

| Time | Camera and dominant image | Editorial beat / transition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 | Coffee macro → sculptural ceramic cup, hovering droplet, soft studio reflections | YOUR BRAIN → ON COFFEE. Fast dive through liquid at 3.5s. |
| 4–10 | Immersive bronze corridor, then real caffeine conformer in macro | CAFFEINE / C₈H₁₀N₄O₂. Atom crosses lens, taking us into vessel. |
| 10–16 | Tracking camera inside a vessel; biconcave cells pass in foreground and background | BLOOD → BRAIN. Aperture brightens into the brain reveal. |
| 16–23 | Orbit a detailed brain; push through cortex into neural/receptor scale | Real caffeine and adenosine topologies; schematic receptor occupancy. BLOCKS / ADENOSINE SIGNALING. |
| 23–31 | Move through the binding pocket into a layered neural forest | LESS SLEEPY → MORE ALERT, with shared letter motion and changing negative space. Activity is a visual metaphor, not electric fuel. |
| 31–39 | Lens relaxes into a molecule and orbital time dial; descending curve draws in | CAFFEINE FADES / SLEEP PRESSURE REMAINS. Conceptual timeline, no universal half-life claim. |
| 39–45 | Orbital dial becomes coffee rim; camera emerges into original studio | COFFEE DOESN’T ERASE TIREDNESS → IT MASKS THE SIGNAL. Return to opening macro. |

Hero stills: ceramic/droplet at 1.5s; vessel fly-through at 13s; anatomical brain at 17.5s; receptor interaction at 21.5s; neural typography at 28s.

Assets: original lathed ceramic, shaded crema surface, procedural biconcave cells/vessel/neural arborization/seven-helix receptor; licensed brain mesh; PubChem molecular topology and conformer coordinates. Scientific models are intentionally stylized and not to scale. Lighting uses generated studio reflections rather than remote HDRI downloads.

Audio: original deterministic 45-second ambient pulse score and synchronized transition/docking effects. No configured TTS credentials or voice asset; narration is explicitly unrecorded. Captions and a cue-timed script carry the full explanation.

Reference inspected at 0.7, 3, 6, 9, and 12 seconds: https://prompt-motion.com/lexnlin-038035 . Observed rapid spatial reframing, a traveling circular motif, strong type/illustration handoffs, and sparse color. No reference artwork, branding, or audio is reused. Audio synchronization could not be assessed from the still inspections.
